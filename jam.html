Artist Background

About Jamaica H. Damas

Hello! My name is Jamaica H. Damas. I am an artist currently taking up a Bachelor of Science in Animation and Multimedia Arts at the University of Northwestern Mindanao.

My creative journey revolves around visual storytelling, character illustration, animation pre-production, graphic design, and traditional sketching. I love combining traditional drawing fundamentals—like value shading and ink line work—with modern digital software tools to bring ideas to life.

I am actively expanding my technical skills in storyboarding, 2D character animation, and digital art, with the vision of working on impactful creative projects in the animation and gaming industry.
